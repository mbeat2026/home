What M-BEAT sets out to do

An initiative by the Centre for Insect Molecular Studies and Environmental Research (CIMSER), PG & Research Department of Advanced Zoology and Biotechnology, Loyola College (Autonomous), Chennai, with Greater Chennai Corporation and AHC Foundation.

  • Identify and eliminate mosquito breeding sources within participating college premises through regular surveillance and source-reduction activities by student-led teams.
  • Establish Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for maintaining mosquito-breeding-free campuses.
  • Promote Behaviour Change Communication (BCC) activities to update college authorities, faculty, students and support staff on vector surveillance, source reduction and preventive measures.
  • Institutionalize periodic inspections, self-monitoring and reporting mechanisms in collaboration with Corporation officials.
  • Recognize and certify colleges that consistently maintain breeding-free premises based on predefined criteria.
  • Develop participating colleges as model institutions that can help replicate this initiative across other educational institutions in Chennai.
